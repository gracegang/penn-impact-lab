Principal Investigator

Grace J. Gang, PhD

Associate Professor, Department of Radiology, University of Pennsylvania

Dr. Grace J. Gang is an associate professor in the Department of Radiology at the University of Pennsylvania. She received a Bachelor of Applied Science and a PhD in Biomedical Engineering from the University of Toronto. Her work focuses on image-quality modeling, assessment, and optimization for advanced X-ray imaging modalities, spanning X-ray detector physics, image reconstruction, system modeling and optimization, and human visual perception. Dr. Gang has received many awards, including first place in the John R. Cameron Young Investigator Symposium, the AAPM Research Seed Grant, the Moses and Sylvia Greenfield Award, the Jack Fowler Junior Investigator Award (2018), and the John Laughlin Early-Career Scientist Award.
