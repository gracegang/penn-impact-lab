Director

Principal Investigator

Grace J. Gang, PhD

Associate Professor, Department of Radiology, University of Pennsylvania
Adjunct Professor, Department of Biomedical Engineering, Johns Hopkins University

Dr. Gang received her PhD in Biomedical Engineering from the University of Toronto and completed her postdoctoral training at Johns Hopkins University, where she served as research faculty before joining Penn Radiology in 2022. Her research builds a quantitative understanding of imaging performance — how physics, algorithms, and the human observer together determine what an image can reveal — and applies it to the design of new CT and cone-beam CT technologies.

She is a Fellow of the AAPM, Chair of the AAPM Cone-Beam CT Subcommittee, a standing member of the NIH CTIS study section, and Associate Editor of Medical Physics.
