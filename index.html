Research areas

Novel Imaging Techniques for Interventional Applications

Novel image acquisition and reconstruction techniques for task-driven imaging and non-circular cone-beam CT orbits for metal artifact reduction.

Spectral Imaging for Diagnostic CT

Detector modeling, material decomposition algorithms, and image-quality modeling for imaging systems with photon-counting and multi-layer detectors.

Deep Learning Image Formation

Algorithms and analysis methods for deep learning reconstruction and post-processing, including approaches that incorporate domain knowledge in learned models.

Physics-Guided Radiomics Standardization

Methods that use prior knowledge of spatial resolution and noise to remove scanner-dependent variability in radiomics features.

Image Quality & Task-Driven Imaging

Image chain modeling and model observers for subjective image quality assessment.

Image Analysis

Image analysis and quantitative feature extraction, including working-angle prediction for neuro-interventional procedures.
