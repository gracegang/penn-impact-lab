Join Us

We welcome highly motivated researchers with strong quantitative and programming skills.

Who we're looking for

We look for researchers with a strong quantitative background and solid programming skills. Our work takes ideas from mathematical theory through simulation and benchtop experiments to the clinic, and the common thread is rigorous, physics-based thinking about how images are formed and used. If you come from engineering, physics, applied math, or computer science and want to apply that foundation to medical imaging, the lab is a good fit.

PhD Students

Prospective PhD students should apply through the University of Pennsylvania graduate programs — most lab students come through Bioengineering. Mention your interest in the IMPACT Lab in your application, and feel free to email Dr. Gang with your CV and a brief description of your research interests.

Postdoctoral Fellows

Qualified candidates are always welcome to contact us. Interested candidates should email Dr. Gang with a CV, a short statement of research interests, and contact information for references.

Undergraduate and Summer Students

We regularly host summer students, including through Penn's TRIP mentoring program. Email Dr. Gang to inquire about opportunities.

How to Apply

Email grace.j.gang@pennmedicine.upenn.edu with:

  • Your CV
  • A brief statement of research interests
  • (Postdocs) References

Strong applicants are comfortable with mathematical modeling and write solid code — typically Python or MATLAB, with PyTorch a plus.
