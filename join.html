Join Us

We welcome researchers with strong quantitative training, clear communication, and an interest in medical imaging technology.

Opportunities

The IMPACT Lab is located in the Department of Radiology at the University of Pennsylvania. Lab members join a collaborative research environment with access to imaging hardware, clinical collaborators, and computing resources for developing and evaluating advanced x-ray imaging technologies.

Strong candidates usually come from biomedical engineering, electrical engineering, physics, applied mathematics, computer science, medical physics, or closely related fields. Prior experience in medical imaging, especially x-ray, CT, or cone-beam CT, is helpful but not required for every role.

Postdoctoral Fellows and Research Scientists

Candidates should hold a PhD in a relevant quantitative field and have strong programming, analytical, and communication skills. Experience with image reconstruction, image analysis, image-quality evaluation, image-chain modeling, hardware integration, or radiomics is especially relevant.

PhD and Master's Students

Prospective graduate students typically apply through University of Pennsylvania graduate programs, most commonly Bioengineering. Students interested in thesis work in the lab should have strong quantitative preparation and be comfortable developing software for research.

Undergraduate and Summer Students

The lab considers undergraduate and summer students when there is a good match between project needs, mentoring bandwidth, and the student's preparation.

How to Apply

Send a CV and a brief description of your research interests. Graduate-degree inquiries should also include a transcript when available.
